privacy policy

effective october 1, 2026 · lightbugs

the short version

lightbugs is a private notebook. your notes, photos and voice recordings are stored on your device — and in your phone’s own backups, if you use them. we do not run accounts, we do not have servers that receive your content, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

what we collect

nothing personal. the app does not ask for your name, email or location. if your phone shares usage & diagnostics with google, google play may share anonymised crash reports with us; these contain no note content.

device permissions

camera — to take a photo you keep as a note in a notebook.

microphone — to record a voice note for a notebook.

photo library — to add photos you pick to a page or a cover, and to save pages you share.

notifications — only if you turn on the evening reminder.

each permission is requested the first time you use that feature and can be revoked any time in android settings.

sharing

when you share moments, lightbugs draws them as images on this phone and hands them to the android share sheet, or straight to instagram stories when you share one page or pick stories, or saves them to your photos. what happens after that is governed by the app you share to. “export my notes” puts every notebook, photo and voice note into one .zip file that is not encrypted; it goes only where you send it, so keep it somewhere private.

network

the only request lightbugs makes asks the app store whether a newer version is out. nothing about you or your notes is sent, and without a connection everything else works the same.

keeping and deleting your notes

your notes stay on this phone until you delete them: remove a moment from its notebook, or erase everything in settings → danger zone → erase all data. deleting the app deletes its data from this phone too. we hold no copy, so there is nothing to ask us to delete. if your phone backs up to your google account, that backup can hold a copy of your notes’ words — not their photos or voice notes — until it is replaced or deleted, and reinstalling the app can bring the words back from it.

children

lightbugs is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

who we are

changes & contact
